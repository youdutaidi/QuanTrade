source scope changes are allowed in this attempt. D3 remote delivery proceeds
independently from its already-frozen snapshot and code.

Add a read-only witness and thin CLI composition. The witness must read one
SQLite transaction; compare all planned code/year tasks against the fixed plan;
check request/run/task ownership and daily-input provenance; verify every raw
SHA256, identity, schema and row count; and check stored derived-row consistency.

visible and unused, with their original bytes retained.

as invalid, and source ambiguities as explicit unresolved field counts. Empty
source years are successful requests, not proof of an absence across vendors.
All outputs must keep economic-ledger and investor-tax flags false.

tampered raw bytes; wrong request ownership; changed normalized numeric value;
and a concurrent writer that commits after the audit establishes its snapshot.

Output evidence uses a new path and refuses overwrite. No market outcomes or
holdout selection, database mutation, external trade, or new source login.
